# Privacy Policy — Routines Timer

**Last updated: October 25, 2026**

## Overview

Routines Timer ("the App") is a Zepp OS mini program that allows you to create, save and run sequences of timers with pause/stop control.

## Data We Collect

The App **does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data**.

## Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App only stores the following information **locally on your watch**, using the device's built-in local storage:

- Timer routines you create (names and durations of each timer in a sequence).
- App preferences (e.g., vibration settings, if applicable).

This data:

- **Never leaves your device.** It is not uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.
- Is not accessible to the developer or any other party.
- Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing the App's data through your device settings.

## Permissions

The App requests the following permission:

- **Local storage** (`device:os.local_storage`) — used exclusively to save your timer routines on your watch so they persist between sessions.

## Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App does **not** use any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published with the App's store listing before the changes take effect.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer through the contact information shown on the App's store page.
